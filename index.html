Loomtale

Loomtale is a private, self-hosted studio that turns stories into animated videos and publishes them to YouTube channels owned by its operator.

It connects to YouTube through the YouTube API Services to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and read channel analytics. It is not offered to the public; only the operator's own channels are connected.
