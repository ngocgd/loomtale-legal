Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Loomtale uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access

  • Basic YouTube channel information (channel ID, title, upload limits), to show which channel is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos, set thumbnails and manage playlists on the connected channel.
  • YouTube Analytics reports for videos uploaded by Loomtale, to show their performance to the operator.

How we use and store it

The data is used only to publish the operator's own videos and show their statistics inside Loomtale. OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted and stored on the operator's own server. Analytics figures are stored there too. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting a channel in Loomtale revokes its token at Google and deletes the stored copy. You can also revoke access at any time on the Google security settings page. Stored analytics are deleted on request within 30 days.
